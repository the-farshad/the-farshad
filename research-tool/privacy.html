Privacy policy

Research tool · Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Research tool accesses when you sign in with your Google account, and what it does with that information.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: files and folders in your account, to create, upload, organize and share your research files.
  • Google Docs: documents in your account, to write and update your research notes and plans.
  • Google Tasks: your task lists, to add and update your research to-dos.

Research tool does not collect any other personal information.

How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the features above, in your own account and at your request. Some notes may be drafted with the help of AI services, which then receive the text of the notes and tasks being drafted.

Storage and sharing

Your files, documents and tasks stay in your Google account. The tool runs on the computer where you install it, which keeps temporary working copies and the sign-in needed to reach your account. Your information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone, except files and folders you choose to share.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files and tasks the tool created can be deleted in Google Drive and Google Tasks like any others.

Changes

This policy may change. The effective date above shows the latest version.
