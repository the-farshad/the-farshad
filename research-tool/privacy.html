Privacy policy

Research tool · Effective October 26, 2026

Research tool is a private tool that the owner of this site uses with their own Google account to organize research. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with that information.

Who uses it

Only the owner uses this tool, with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: files and folders in the owner's account, to create, upload, organize and share research files.
  • Google Docs: documents in the owner's account, to write and update research notes and plans.
  • Google Tasks: the owner's task lists, to add and update research to-dos.

How the information is used

The information is used only to create, update and organize research material in the owner's own account. Some notes are drafted with the help of AI tools the owner uses, which may receive the text of those notes and tasks for that purpose.

Storage and sharing

Files, documents and tasks stay in the owner's Google account. The tool runs on the owner's own computers, which keep temporary working copies and the sign-in needed to reach the account. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone, except folders the owner chooses to share.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files and tasks the tool created can be deleted in Google Drive and Google Tasks like any others.
