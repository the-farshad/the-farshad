Research tool

Organize research material with Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Tasks.

Research tool helps researchers keep their work in order in their own Google account. After you sign in with Google, it works only with your own files, documents and task lists.

What it does

  • Google Drive: builds and maintains a research library: reports, reading lists, papers, and study material such as slides.
  • Google Docs: writes research notes and daily plans as documents.
  • Google Tasks: keeps a research to-do list up to date.
