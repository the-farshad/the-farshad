Research tool

A private tool for organizing research material in Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Tasks.

Research tool is a set of scripts that the owner of this site uses to organize research in their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else, and only the owner signs in to it.

What it does

  • Google Drive: keeps a research library in order: reports, reading lists, published papers, and study material such as slides.
  • Google Docs: writes daily research notes and plans as documents.
  • Google Tasks: keeps a research to-do list up to date.

It works only with files, documents and task lists in the owner's account, and with folders the owner chooses to share.
