Privacy policy

PhD research tools · Effective October 26, 2026

PhD research tools is a private tool that I, Farshad Ghorbanishovaneh, use with my own Google account to organize my doctoral research. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with that information.

Who uses it

Only I use this tool, with my own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: files and folders in my account, to create, upload, organize and share my research files.
  • Google Docs: documents in my account, to write and update my research notes and plans.
  • Google Tasks: my task lists, to add and update my research to-dos.

How the information is used

The information is used only to create, update and organize my own research material in my own account. Some notes are drafted with the help of AI tools that I use, which may receive the text of my own research notes and tasks for that purpose.

Storage and sharing

My files, documents and tasks stay in my Google account. The tool runs on my own computers, which keep temporary working copies and the sign-in needed to reach my account. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone, except folders I choose to share with my other accounts.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files and tasks the tool created can be deleted in Google Drive and Google Tasks like any others.
