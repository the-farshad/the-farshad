PhD research tools

A private tool by Farshad Ghorbanishovaneh, PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Wyoming.

PhD research tools is a set of scripts I use to organize my own doctoral research in my own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else, and only I sign in to it.

What it does

  • Google Drive: keeps my research library in order: my reports, reading lists, the published papers I study, and study material such as slides.
  • Google Docs: writes my daily research notes and plans as documents.
  • Google Tasks: keeps my research to-do list up to date.

It works only with files, documents and task lists in my own account, and with folders I choose to share with my other accounts.
